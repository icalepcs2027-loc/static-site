Mito is a historic city with sites associated with the Tokugawa family and traditional Japanese gardens. Alongside Kairakuen Garden and the Mito Castle Ruins, Art Tower Mito presents contemporary art, music, and theater.

# About Mito

Mito is the capital of Ibaraki Prefecture. During the Edo period (1603–1868), Japan was governed by a warrior government headed by the shogun. The Mito Tokugawa family, which ruled Mito, was a branch of the family that supplied successive shoguns. The city developed around their castle.

Tokugawa Mitsukuni, the second lord of Mito, is known as “Mito Komon.” Television period dramas based on him are widely known in Japan. He is also popularly said to have been the first person in Japan to eat ramen.[^ramen-history]

Tokugawa Nariaki, the ninth lord, founded Kodokan, a school for samurai, and Kairakuen Garden. For more information, see [Mito City's introduction to the Mito Tokugawa family (Japanese)](https://www.city.mito.lg.jp/site/education/4410.html).

[^ramen-history]: There are also records of a Chinese noodle dish called “keitaimen” being eaten in Japan in 1488, before Mitsukuni's time, so accounts of ramen's origins differ. See [“The History of Ramen in Japan,” Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum (Japanese)](https://www.raumen.co.jp/rapedia/study_history/).

Kairakuen Garden and the Mito Castle Ruins are each about a 30-minute walk from the conference venue, Mito Civic Center.

# Main Attractions

## Kairakuen Garden

Kairakuen is a garden established by Tokugawa Nariaki in 1842. It is one of Japan's best-known gardens, particularly famous for its plum groves. Unfortunately, the conference in October falls outside the plum blossom season.

The garden also has bamboo groves and cedar woods. Kobuntei, a traditional Japanese building, offers views of Lake Senba.

If you have more time, consider a walk around the neighboring Tokiwa Shrine or Lake Senba.

- **From Mito Civic Center:** About 25–30 minutes on foot to the Omotemon main gate area. The garden has several entrances; check the [official access guide (Japanese)](https://ibaraki-kairakuen.jp/access/) for the entrance closest to the places you plan to visit.
- **From Mito Station:** Take a bus toward Kairakuen from the north exit, about 15–20 minutes. Depending on the bus stop and entrance, allow a few more minutes on foot. See the [Mito Tourism and Convention Association's guide (Japanese)](https://mitokoumon.com/facility/historic/kairakuen/) for bus stops and routes.
- **Official website:** [Kairakuen Garden](https://ibaraki-kairakuen.jp/)

## Art Tower Mito

Art Tower Mito is a cultural complex adjacent to Mito Civic Center, the conference venue. It has a concert hall, a theater, and a contemporary art gallery, hosting concerts, plays, and exhibitions.

The 100-meter tower, clearly visible from the venue, was built to commemorate the 100th anniversary of Mito's incorporation as a city. Its spiral form consists of stacked geometric units with triangular faces, clad in titanium. Inside, a glass elevator takes visitors to an observation room about 86 meters above the ground, with views across the city. See the [official tower guide](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/english/tower/) for its structure and admission information.

Check the official website for exhibition and performance schedules.

- **From Mito Civic Center:** Directly adjacent to the venue. Approach Art Tower Mito from the plaza side.
- **From Mito Station:** Take a bus from stops 4–7 at the north exit bus terminal, about 10 minutes, and get off at “Izumi-cho 1-chome.” Walk about 2 minutes from there. See the [official access guide](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/english/access/).
- **Official website:** [Art Tower Mito (Japanese)](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/) / [Art Tower Mito (English)](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/english/)

## Mito Castle Ruins

The Mito Castle Ruins occupy the site of the Mito Tokugawa family's castle. No castle keep or palace remains, but visitors can see gates, turrets, earthworks, and moats. Nearby is [Kodokan](https://kodokan-ibaraki.jp/guide/), a school where samurai studied academic subjects and martial arts.

- **From Mito Civic Center:** About 25–30 minutes on foot to the Otemon Gate area. Alternatively, take a bus toward Mito Station from “Izumi-cho 1-chome,” then walk about 10 minutes from the station's north exit.
- **From Mito Station:** About 10 minutes on foot from the north exit to Otemon Gate. See [Mito City's Otemon Gate guide (Japanese)](https://www.city.mito.lg.jp/site/education/5371.html).
- **Official tourism information:** [Mito Castle Ruins — Mito Tourism and Convention Association (Japanese)](https://mitokoumon.com/facility/historic/mitojoato/)

Restaurants near the venue serve sushi, soba noodles, tempura, tonkatsu, grilled eel, and other Japanese dishes. For lunch close to the venue, the restaurant floor on the ninth floor of Keisei Department Store, across the street, is a convenient option. Long-established restaurants serving regional dishes and eel are also within walking distance.

| [Hitachi Aki Soba Izumi](https://www.mitokeisei.co.jp/shop/3601/) | Hot and cold soba noodles made with buckwheat from Ibaraki, and tempura. | Ninth floor of Keisei Department Store, across the street. |

| [Mito Sansui](https://www.sansui-mito.com/) | A long-established restaurant serving Ibaraki's regional cuisine, including monkfish dishes, pot-cooked rice, and set meals. | Izumi-cho 2-chome, a few minutes on foot. |

Allow time to reach the restaurant floor and for queues during busy periods. For eel dishes or multi-course meals, book ahead and allow time for a longer lunch or dinner after the conference. Check each restaurant's official information for opening hours, closing days, and seasonal menus.

For more restaurants, see [Mito Civic Center's neighborhood dining guide (Japanese)](https://www.mito-hall.jp/guide/gourmet/). It also includes dishes made with natto, Mito's local fermented soybean specialty.

## Souvenirs

Popular souvenirs include Mito's traditional sweets **Mito no Ume** and **Yoshiwara Denchu**, and **hoshi-imo**, dried sweet potatoes from Ibaraki. Plum wine and locally brewed sake are also available. These products are sold in the basement food hall of Keisei Department Store and around Mito Station. For products and shops, see [Visit MITO, Mito City's official travel guide](https://visitmito.jp/en/).

## Traditional Crafts

**Suifu chochin** are traditional lanterns made from washi paper stretched over a bamboo frame. “Suifu” is another name for Mito. In addition to lanterns for festivals, makers produce designs for indoor lighting.

水戸市は茨城県の県庁所在地です。江戸時代（1603～1868年）、日本では将軍を頂点とする武家政権が国を統治していました。水戸を治めた水戸徳川家は、将軍を代々出した徳川家の分家です。水戸のまちは、その城を中心に発展しました。

# 主要観光地

## 偕楽園

偕楽園は、1842年に徳川斉昭が開いた庭園です。日本でもっとも有名な庭園の一つで、特に美しい梅林で知られています。(残念ながら、会議が開かれる10月はシーズン外です。)  
園内には竹林や杉林があり、伝統的な日本建築の好文亭からは千波湖を見渡せます。

時間に余裕があれば、隣接する常磐神社や千波湖周辺も散策してみてください。

- **水戸市民会館から：** 偕楽園の表門方面まで徒歩約25～30分が目安です。園内には複数の入口があるため、訪ねたい場所に合わせて[公式アクセス案内](https://ibaraki-kairakuen.jp/access/)で入口を確認してください。
- **水戸駅から：** 北口から偕楽園方面行きのバスで約15～20分。降車する停留所や入口によって、さらに数分の徒歩が必要です。乗り場と経路は[水戸観光コンベンション協会の案内](https://mitokoumon.com/facility/historic/kairakuen/)をご覧ください。
- **公式サイト：** [日本三名園 偕楽園](https://ibaraki-kairakuen.jp/)

## 水戸芸術館

水戸芸術館は、会場の水戸市民会館に隣接する文化施設です。コンサートホール、劇場、現代美術ギャラリーがあり、コンサートや演劇公演、美術展を開催しています。

会場からよく見える高さ100メートルのタワーは、水戸市制100周年を記念して建てられました。三角形の面を持つ立体を積み重ねた、らせん状の形とチタン製の外装が特徴です。内部にはガラス張りのエレベーターがあり、地上約86メートルの展望室から水戸の市街地を見渡せます。構造や入場の案内は、[公式のタワー紹介](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/tower/)をご覧ください。

展覧会や公演の日程は公式サイトをご確認ください。

- **水戸市民会館から：** 会場に隣接しています。広場側から水戸芸術館へお進みください。
- **水戸駅から：** 北口バスターミナル4～7番のりばからバスで約10分、「泉町一丁目」下車、徒歩約2分。[公式アクセス案内](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/access/)をご参照ください。
- **公式サイト：** [水戸芸術館](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/)／[Art Tower Mito（英語）](https://www.arttowermito.or.jp/english/)

## 水戸城跡

水戸城跡は、水戸徳川家の城があった場所です。天守や御殿はありませんが、門や櫓、土塁や堀などが見学できます。近くには、武士が学問や武術を学んだ学校[「弘道館」](https://kodokan-ibaraki.jp/guide/)もあります。

- **水戸市民会館から：** 大手門周辺まで徒歩約25～30分が目安です。バスの場合は、「泉町一丁目」から水戸駅方面のバスで水戸駅へ向かい、北口から徒歩約10分です。
- **水戸駅から：** 北口から大手門まで徒歩約10分。[水戸市の大手門案内](https://www.city.mito.lg.jp/site/education/5371.html)をご参照ください。
- **公式観光情報：** [水戸城跡（水戸観光コンベンション協会）](https://mitokoumon.com/facility/historic/mitojoato/)

## お土産

水戸の和菓子**「水戸の梅」**や**「吉原殿中」**、茨城県の名産である**干し芋**などが定番です。梅酒や地元の日本酒もあります。京成百貨店の地下食品売場や水戸駅周辺で販売されています。商品や販売店の情報は、[水戸市公式観光ガイド「Visit MITO」](https://visitmito.jp/en/)をご覧ください。

## 伝統工芸

**水府提灯（すいふちょうちん）**は、竹の骨組みに和紙を貼った伝統的な照明です。「水府」は水戸の別称です。祭りなどで使う提灯のほか、室内用の照明としてデザインされた製品も作られています。
