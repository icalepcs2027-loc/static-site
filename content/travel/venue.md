ICALEPCS 2027 will be held at **[Mito Civic Center](https://www.mito-hall.jp/english/)** in Mito, Ibaraki, Japan.
It is adjacent to Art Tower Mito and across the street from Keisei Department Store, with restaurants and shops nearby.

**Address:** 1-7-1 Izumi-cho, Mito, Ibaraki 310-0026, Japan

# Access from Mito Station

For travel from Tokyo, Haneda Airport, or Narita Airport to Mito, see [Getting to Mito]({{< relref "travel/city.md" >}}).

## By Bus

Take a bus from **stops 4–7 at the north exit** of Mito Station and get off at **Izumi-cho 1-chome (泉町一丁目)**. The ride takes about **5–10 minutes**, depending on traffic. The venue is about a **1-minute walk** from the bus stop.

## On Foot

The venue is about a **20-minute walk** from Mito Station's north exit.

See the [official access guide (Japanese)](https://www.mito-hall.jp/access/) for routes and a map of the surrounding area.
