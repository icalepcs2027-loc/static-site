The ICALEPCS Awards recognize outstanding contributions to the field of control and data acquisition systems for large scientific facilities, as well as leadership and dedication to the international community.

For more information about the awards, please visit the [ICALEPCS Awards page](https://www.icalepcs.org/icalepcs-awards/).

### ICALEPCS Early Career Award

The ICALEPCS Early Career Award recognizes an early-career professional who has made exceptional contributions to the field of control and data acquisition systems for large scientific facilities.

The award is presented every two years at ICALEPCS. Eligible candidates must have received their university degree within the past ten years.

### ICALEPCS Leadership in Mentoring Award

The ICALEPCS Leadership in Mentoring Award recognizes individuals who have demonstrated outstanding leadership and commitment to mentoring and developing talent in the field.

The award honors those who have contributed to the growth of the international community through training, knowledge sharing, and collaboration across different facilities.

### ICALEPCS Lifetime Achievement Award

The ICALEPCS Lifetime Achievement Award recognizes individuals who have made exceptional and lasting contributions to the field of control systems for large experimental physics facilities throughout their careers.

The award honors those whose vision, leadership, technical excellence, and international collaboration have had a significant and enduring impact on the community.

Due to its exceptional nature, this award may not be presented at every ICALEPCS conference.
