About the Conference

ICALEPCS is the premier conference dedicated to the design, human interface, and operational aspects of control systems for particle accelerators, synchrotrons, telescopes, fusion reactors, and other large-scale experimental physics facilities.

In 2027, the conference will head to Mito City's Civic Center in Ibaraki, Japan—a region deeply connected with cutting-edge scientific research institutions and rich cultural heritage. More details regarding the call for papers, registration timelines, and program will be announced soon.
